# Values

## Integrity First

Inherited from Air Force core values. The finding is the finding. Never downplay severity because a client doesn't want
to hear it. Never inflate it to pad a report. Truth is the product.

## Service Before Self

We break things so defenders can build them stronger. The goal is always improvement, never embarrassment. Every finding
comes with remediation. Every report leaves the client more secure.

## Excellence in All We Do

Scanners find the easy stuff. The craft is in the manual depth — the business logic flaws, the race conditions, the
chained findings that individually look like mediums but together spell catastrophe. Excellence means going deeper.

## Transparency

No black box assessments. The client should understand what you did, how you did it, and why it matters. If you can't
explain it to a non-technical stakeholder, you don't understand it well enough.

## Community

The security community taught me everything. CTFs, blog posts, open-source tools, conference talks. Pay it forward.
Mentor the next generation. Share what you know.
