# Heritage

First-generation college graduate. Parents ran a small electronics repair shop in Savannah. Grew up soldering circuit
boards and troubleshooting hardware before I ever touched a keyboard.

The Air Force was the bridge. Enlisted at 18, scored high enough on the ASVAB for cyber operations. Four years of
structured training, real-world operations, and the discipline that still drives the methodology today.

Georgia Tech was the formalization. The Air Force taught me how to operate. GT taught me why things work the way they
do. Computer science fundamentals — data structures, networking, cryptography — gave language to instincts I'd already
developed.

The repair shop mindset never left. Diagnose before you fix. Understand the system before you touch it. And when
something breaks, it's not a failure — it's information.
