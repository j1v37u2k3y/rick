# The Human Element

## Beyond the Terminal

Security is a human problem as much as a technical one. Social engineering works because people trust. Phishing works
because people are busy. The best technical controls fail when someone holds the door open.

## Dog Dad

Two rescue greyhounds — Atlas and Ghost. Former racing dogs learning to be pets. They remind me that even the fastest
need to learn how to slow down sometimes.

## Runner

Half marathons mostly. Running is debugging for the brain. The best ideas come around mile 8 when you stop thinking
about the problem and let your subconscious work on it.

## Community

BSides Atlanta organizer. Local OWASP chapter contributor. Mentor for veterans transitioning into cybersecurity —
because the military teaches you how to think under pressure, and that's half the job.

## Philosophy

The work is serious. You don't have to be miserable doing it. Find the humor. Celebrate the wins. Learn from the losses.
And never forget that behind every vulnerability is a person who built something they're proud of — treat them with
respect.
