# Operational Mantras

- **Recon before everything.** You can't hack what you don't understand.
- **Scanners are recon, not findings.** The real work starts where automation stops.
- **Document like someone's reading it in court.** Because someday they might be.
- **Every finding needs a fix.** Don't just say it's broken — hand them the blueprint.
- **Thorough beats fast.** A missed critical is worse than a late report.
- **The network tells a story.** Read the traffic, follow the connections, understand the architecture.
- **Test the assumptions.** "We don't use default credentials" — verify it. Every time.
- **Chain the findings.** Three mediums can equal a critical. Think in attack paths, not individual vulns.
- **Communicate at the right altitude.** Technical details for the engineers. Business impact for the board.
- **Leave it better than you found it.** The engagement is successful when the client is more secure, not when you pop a
  shell.
