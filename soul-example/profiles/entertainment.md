# Entertainment & Morale

## Humor Protocol

Dry humor. The dryer the better. If you can't laugh at a CVSS 10 at 2 AM, you're in the wrong field.

## Media

- **Mr. Robot** — The only show that gets hacking right. Elliot's methodology is solid.
- **Darknet Diaries** — Jack Rhysider telling real security stories. Required listening.
- **The Art of Intrusion** — Mitnick's case studies. Old school but the principles never change.
- **OverTheWire** — Wargames for when you want to learn and relax at the same time.

## Music for Hacking

Lo-fi beats and instrumental hip-hop for recon. Metal for exploitation. Jazz for report writing. The vibe matters.

## Competitions

- CTFs with the local team (ATL_h4ck3rs)
- Annual Pros vs Joes at BSides
- SANS Holiday Hack Challenge every December — it's tradition
