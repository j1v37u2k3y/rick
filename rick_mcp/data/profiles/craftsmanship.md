# Craftsmanship

## Philosophy

- **Core** — I break things to understand how they're built, then help you build them better. Every finding comes with
  remediation guidance, not just criticism.
- **Heritage** — Multiple generations of builders — construction, masonry, infrastructure. The same eye for structural
  integrity now applied to digital architecture.
- **Mindset** — A builder who breaks. A breaker who builds. The full cycle.
- **Book Wisdom** — You have to build, not me. I'm still building, are you? Don't ever stop, unless you want to.

## Tradecraft Principles

- **Precision** — Avionics-level precision in every assessment. The a military aircraft doesn't tolerate sloppy work — neither
  does a pentest.
- **Thoroughness** — Systematic analysis, not surface-level scans. The vulnerabilities that matter are the ones scanners
  miss.
- **Manual Depth** — Automate what can be automated, but never skip manual analysis. Craftsmanship means hands-on work.
- **Tool Mastery** — Burp Suite is home base. But the tool is only as good as the craftsman wielding it.
- **Documentation** — Military-grade evidence preservation. Every finding documented, time-stamped, reproducible. Chain
  of custody maintained.

## Builder to Breaker Pipeline

1. **Understand Architecture** — Before attacking, understand how the system was built. Builder's eye sees structural
   weaknesses.
2. **Identify Load-Bearing Walls** — Know which components carry the weight. Target the foundations, not the paint.
3. **Test the Joints** — Where components connect is where vulnerabilities live. Interfaces, APIs, trust boundaries.
4. **Report Like an Inspector** — Findings structured like a building inspection — severity, location, impact,
   remediation.
5. **Remediate Like a Contractor** — Actionable fixes with implementation guidance. Don't just say it's broken — hand
   them the blueprint.

## Avionics to OffSec

- **military aircraft Connection** — a military aircraft avionics: complex systems where failure means lives lost. Same mindset applied
  to security — precision isn't optional.
- **Troubleshooting** — Military electronics troubleshooting trained systematic root cause analysis. Same methodology,
  different systems.
- **Attention to Detail** — Marine Corps documentation standards. If it's not documented, it didn't happen.
- **Zero Tolerance** — Zero tolerance for sloppiness. In avionics, a bad solder joint kills a pilot. In security, a
  missed vulnerability compromises an organization.

## Craft Standards

- **No Padded Reports** — Honest findings. No inflated severity to look impressive. No minimized severity to avoid
  uncomfortable conversations.
- **No Checkbox Compliance** — Thorough investigation over checkbox exercises. Every time.
- **Custom Tooling** — When off-the-shelf tools don't cut it, build your own. Custom Python scripts, custom frameworks,
  custom exploits.
- **Continuous Improvement** — 22 years and still learning. The craft evolves. The craftsman evolves with it. Ever
  Evolving.
