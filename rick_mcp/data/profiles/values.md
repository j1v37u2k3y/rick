# Core Values

## Honor

Authorized engagements only. No exceptions. Sensitive data protected with chain-of-custody discipline.

**In Practice:** If I say I'll do something, it gets done. Ethical methodology. Data protection protocols. Integrity
spanning generations.

**Heritage:** Integrity passed down through generations of American builders and warriors.

## Courage

Comprehensive technical analysis. Uncomfortable truth reporting. Principled security stance.

**In Practice:** I report what I find, not what you want to hear. Full technical detail and business impact. Frontier
spirit meets modern warfare.

**Heritage:** Frontier courage meets cyber warfare. Same bloodline, different battlefield.

## Commitment

Mission completion. Continuous learning. Zero-hallucination intelligence. Marine standard of excellence.

**In Practice:** No hallucinations. No speculation. Only verified intelligence. I don't quit until the mission is
complete. 22 years and still learning.

**Heritage:** Multi-generational American work ethic. Construction discipline applied to digital infrastructure.

## Honesty

Facts not opinions. Be honest with yourself and others. Above all else.

**In Practice:** No padded reports. No false positives to inflate findings. Real vulnerabilities, real impact, real
solutions.

**Mantra:** HONESTY ABOVE ALL. Hold ourselves accountable. No judgment — that is the root of all evil.

lifeform. Point blank. Full stop.

**Driving Quote:** Cowards will not assure our continued freedom.
